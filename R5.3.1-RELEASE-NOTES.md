# Freqtrade-Hedge R5.3.1 Safety Closure

## Release position

R5.3.1 is a safety-closure patch over the R5.3.0 Authoritative Execution release candidate.
It does not unlock unattended mainnet trading. Mainnet writes remain locked pending real
user-stream evidence, clean Windows diagnostics, and 24/72-hour observation evidence.

## Fixed in this patch

1. Real user-stream evidence is mandatory for authoritative write composition. R4, R2.4
   and user-stream reports are account-bound, freshness checked and fail closed.
2. Unresolved recovery blocks composition. UNKNOWN/PREPARED/SUBMITTING orders are no
   longer represented as zero by construction.
3. REST facts and the account risk portfolio are rebuilt after Income projection and
   order recovery before any executable composition is returned.
4. Binance Income is the single financial daily-loss authority. Fill/inventory projection
   and the 1-minute production session no longer increment the SQL loss budget.
5. Single-writer lease renewal is enabled and exact fencing remains checked at the final
   exchange side-effect boundary.
6. The execution engine uses the mutable HedgeRiskRuntime readiness monitor rather than
   AlwaysReadyGate.
7. SQL budget is a required runtime dependency; alternate stores and missing submit
   reservation coverage are rejected.
8. Stage Permit and emergency Permit consumption are atomic under their issuing authority.
9. Decision cursors are completed only after write-count, loss-budget and post-cycle
   account validation succeeds.
10. Emergency CLI authorization uses a live conservative price and rejects a caller price
    deviating by more than two percent.
11. Windows offline acceptance explicitly closes SQLite/SQLAlchemy resources.
12. The secret scanner no longer reports its own rule literal as an embedded private key.
13. R5.3 compatibility and full Hedge regression tests are updated for authoritative
    persistence and same-millisecond monotonic user-stream semantics.

## Deliberately still locked/deferred

- Real mainnet execute-plan and emergency-reduce are not enabled by installation.
- 24/72-hour unattended evidence remains pending.
- Budget settlement after a partially successful multi-action batch remains conservative:
  the prepared batch reservation is confirmed after any exchange side effect.
- The authoritative CLI still builds a bounded command composition, not a completed
  unattended long-running trading daemon.

## Exchange access during installation

NONE. The installer and diagnostics do not read credentials and do not call Binance.
